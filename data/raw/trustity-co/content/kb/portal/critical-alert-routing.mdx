# Critical alert routing

Owners and Admins configure critical alert destinations under **Administration → Alerts** (or the enterprise alerts settings page in your tenant).

## What gets routed

Critical operational signals - for example agent-offline style events and high-severity VisionX / HostGuard outcomes your org enables - can be forwarded to:

- Email recipients (requires Trustity outbound email configuration). Messages are HTML with a plain-text fallback and include structured details plus a Cloud Portal deep link when available.
- Webhook URLs your SOC or ticketing system exposes

## Configure routing

1. Sign in as Owner or Admin.
2. Open the critical alerts settings page.
3. Enable email and/or webhook destinations.
4. Enter verified recipient addresses or HTTPS webhook URLs.
5. Save and send a controlled test if available.
6. Confirm delivery in your mailbox or webhook consumer logs.

## Operational expectations

- Alert delivery is best-effort with retries; it is not a guaranteed pager platform.
- Deduplication and atomic claim improvements are on the roadmap - expect possible duplicates during outages.
- Pair portal alerts with SIEM / syslog forwarding when you need durable SOC ingestion; see [Log forwarding](/kb/portal/log-forwarding).
