# Evidence retention and audit integrity

Owners and Admins manage retention under **Administration → Retention** (and related audit export actions).

## Retention preferences

1. Open retention settings.
2. Set evidence and audit retention windows that match your legal and security policy.
3. Use **preview** to see how many rows a purge would affect before enabling automated cleanup.
4. Save only after change approval.

Live audit rows may be archived into a dedicated archive table by retention jobs. The database enforces append-only behavior for the live audit stream. Formal WORM storage and a concurrency-safe tamper-evident chain are not GA claims.

## Audit JSONL export

1. Open Audit Logs or Security Assurance export actions available to your role.
2. Export JSONL for the requested window.
3. Capture the `X-Trustity-Export-SHA256` response header with the file.
4. Store the file and hash together for evidence packages.

The SHA-256 header is **integrity metadata** for the exported bytes. It is **not** a cryptographic digital signature or third-party attestation.
