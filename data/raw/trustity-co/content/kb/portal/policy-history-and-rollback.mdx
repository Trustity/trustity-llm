# Policy history and rollback

Policy changes for entitled modules are recorded so Owners and Admins can review recent history and roll back when needed.

## Review history

1. Open **Policies → History** (or the policy history panel for the module you manage).
2. Select a snapshot and review who changed what and when.
3. Confirm the target module and scope before any restore.

## Roll back

1. Choose a prior known-good snapshot.
2. Confirm the rollback prompt.
3. Wait for agents to refresh policy on their next heartbeat cycle.
4. Validate on a canary device before declaring the fleet restored.

## Tips

- Prefer deployment rings for risky changes instead of relying only on rollback; see [Pilot to production rollout](/kb/getting-started/pilot-to-production-rollout).
- Record the reason for rollback in your internal change ticket.
- Review **Audit Logs** after rollback.
