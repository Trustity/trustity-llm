Secure delivery 

Trustity SecSend 

Sensitive documents, delivered securely - unlocked only by the right person. 

When banks, insurers, and regulated teams need to send contracts, statements, or personal files, email alone is not enough. SecSend wraps each package in strong encryption and requires an SMS code before the recipient can open it - so delivery stays fast for customers and safer for your organization. 

Talk to sales Product site 

Why teams choose SecSend 

Protected before it leaves your team. Packages are encrypted on the sender’s side. What travels is a sealed package - not an open attachment sitting in inboxes. 

The recipient proves who they are. A secure link arrives by email. Opening it requires a one-time SMS code to the phone number your team provided - adding a second channel of verification. 

Built for real customer journeys. Reusable templates, clear staff roles, branded sending identity, and bilingual support - so operations can move quickly without cutting corners on security. 

Part of the Trustity portfolio - complementary to endpoint and edge protections, focused on secure outbound delivery.
