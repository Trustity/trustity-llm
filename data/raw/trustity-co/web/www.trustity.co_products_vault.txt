Vault & PAM 

Rotate local privileged passwords. Vault what operators need. 

Standing local admin passwords are a classic breach path. Trustity Vault/PAM rotates local accounts on managed endpoints through TAO, reports results to the Cloud Portal, and keeps vaulted material under zero-knowledge wrapping so Trustity cannot read customer secrets. 

Request a Demo Request Access Read docs 

Why Vault & PAM? 

Local account focus. Rotate local privileged accounts on the device - domain AD passwords are out of scope by design. 

Zero-knowledge vault. Split-key wrapping keeps vaulted credentials unreadable to Trustity operators. 

Portal workflows. Schedule rotation, force-rotate after suspected compromise, and audit who accessed what.
