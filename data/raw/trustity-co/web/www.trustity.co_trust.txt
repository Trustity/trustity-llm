Data flow (high level) 

Endpoints run the TAO agent (and GenGuard in the browser where deployed). Policy and telemetry travel to the Trustity Cloud Portal over TLS. Operators manage fleet policy there - not on this marketing site. 

1 

Endpoint / browser 

TAO enforces VisionX, HostGuard, and local PAM rotation. GenGuard applies browser policy where installed. VisionX detection is designed to run on the device. 

2 

Encrypted channel 

Agent and portal APIs use TLS in transit. Cloud databases and object storage use provider-managed encryption at rest with industry-standard cryptography. 

3 

Cloud Portal (tenant-isolated) 

Each customer organization is isolated. Admins see only their tenant’s devices, policies, and audit events. 

4 

Vaulted credentials (ZK scope) 

Vault wrapping keys are designed so Trustity cannot read customer vault secrets in cleartext. This does not apply to every telemetry field in the portal. 

Security by design 

Least privilege, tenant isolation, and zero-knowledge vault design for secrets that customers entrust to the portal. 

Encryption in transit and at rest 

TLS for portal and agent traffic. Provider-managed encryption at rest for cloud databases and storage, using industry-standard cryptography. 

Zero-knowledge vault (scoped) 

Applies to vaulted secrets / credentials under split-key wrapping - not to every telemetry field in the portal. 

Monitoring & disclosure 

Operational monitoring plus a documented channel at security@trustity.co. Researchers can also use /.well-known/security.txt.

Report a vulnerability 

If you found a potential issue in Trustity systems, contact us promptly. Do not include customer secrets or exploit payloads in public channels. Testing is limited to systems you are authorized to assess; denial-of-service and social engineering are out of scope.
