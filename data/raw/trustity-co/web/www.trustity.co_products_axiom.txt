Edge security 

Trustity Axiom 

Secure SMB and DNS at the edge - without agents on every endpoint. 

Axiom is Trustity’s edge security product for file-server and DNS traffic. It places an inline SMB reverse proxy in front of your shares and a policy-driven DNS security node inside your network, with centralized management, offline licensing, and air-gap-ready operation. 

Visit Trustity Axiom Product site 

Why Trustity Axiom? 

Inline SMB protection. Sit between users and file servers. Inspect write streams, apply reputation policy, and keep evidence without deploying endpoint agents for this path. 

DNS security gateway. Forward, cache, and enforce domain policy close to the traffic - including air-gap-friendly block pages. 

Part of the Trustity portfolio. Sold and licensed as its own product (axmapp.com), alongside Trustity Cloud Portal endpoint controls like VisionX and GenGuard. 

Licensing, customer portal, and node management for Axiom remain on the Axiom product surfaces - not the Trustity Cloud Portal.
