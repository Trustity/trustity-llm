Visual DLP 

VisionX 

DLP that stops what others miss: visual-channel screen capture. 

Traditional DLP blocks copy-paste and USB. When users can't copy, they photograph or record the screen. VisionX closes that attack surface - detecting phones, cameras, lenses, and similar imaging devices with Trustity VisionX Model running fully on the endpoint inside TAO, capturing evidence, and locking the workstation when policy requires it. 

Request a Demo Request Access Read docs 

Why VisionX? 

Closes the visual gap. Standard DLP doesn't see screen photography or lens-based capture. VisionX does. 

Trustity VisionX Model. Our own on-device detector ships with TAO 1.0.34+, dual with stock YOLO, tuned for visual-channel risk at the desk. 

Speed you can tune. Portal Lock response - Faster, Balanced, or More accurate - lets operators trade lock latency for confirmation strictness. 

Evidence & unlock control. Capture proof of violations and require an admin unlock code before work continues.
