# Get access to Developer Central

Developer Central is **not** open self-registration. Only people from Trustity customer organizations are invited after review.

## Request access

1. Open **Request access** on trustity.dev.
2. Enter your work email, company name, and **organization license key**.
3. Optionally add your role and why you need docs/API guidance.
4. Wait for Trustity to review. If approved, you receive an **invitation email** to set a password.
5. Sign in and open **Docs**.

## Where to find your organization license key

1. Sign in to the Trustity Cloud Portal at [trustity.io](https://trustity.io) as an organization owner or admin.
2. Open **Deployment** (deployment / license section for your tenant).
3. Copy the **organization license key** shown there.
4. Paste it into the access request form on trustity.dev.

Treat the license key like a secret. Do not share it in public channels or tickets unless Support asks through a secure path.

## Security expectations

- This account is a **developer identity** for documentation and the customer community — separate from Cloud Portal operator logins.
- Do **not** paste agent secrets, vault material, or customer credentials into profiles or comments.
- Prefer a unique password for Developer Central even if MFA is not yet required.

## Profile

Update display name and company under **Account**. Profile data lives only in the Developer Central project — not in the Cloud Portal tenant database.
